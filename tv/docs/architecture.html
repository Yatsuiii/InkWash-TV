The brush

The remote as an ink brush: 16 clumps of bristles, pressure, ink load, a stroke washed out with Back.

The ink

WebGL2 rice paper: bleeding, drying, flying white; four inks; fewer wet steps on a slow TV. Told, it wakes up in their world's place.

Feel it again

The painting comes back stroke by stroke in their world's place; the dial marks lighter or heavier, calmer or more stirred, and the sky feels it.

The harness: the storyteller's hands, and its limits

Its tools run here: look · strokes · recall · thing · night · ask · tell · remember. Every telling is held to their evidence; nothing scary reaches a child, in code; their taste picks between a line and its alternative.

Kept on the TV, and opened first

Each painter's world, by seal, in a place written from its story: every chapter, its painting and its clip; each thing, how it looks and felt, what is true of it; their book. Forget a thing, and what grew from it goes.
